# PRD: Appointment Reminders (fictional)

**Status:** Sample | **Owner:** Product

## Problem
Patients miss appointments, which wastes clinic time and delays care.

## Goal
Reduce no-shows by reminding patients and making confirmation easy.

## Users
Patients, reception staff.

## Scope
- In: SMS and push reminders, confirm/cancel link
- Out: payments, insurance checks

## Requirements
1. Reminders at 24h and 2h before the visit.
2. Confirm or cancel in one tap.
3. Staff see confirmation status on the schedule.

## Risks
Message delivery failures, patient consent for messaging, clinic staffing for freed slots.

## Open questions
Which channel do patients prefer? What reminder timing works best?
