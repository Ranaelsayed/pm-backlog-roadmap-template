# Sample Backlog (fictional product: "CareBook", a clinic appointment app)

Priority score = (Impact x Confidence) / Effort. Each factor is rated 1-5.

| ID | User story | Impact | Confidence | Effort | Score |
|----|-----------|--------|------------|--------|-------|
| B-01 | As a patient, I want appointment reminders so that I do not miss visits. | 5 | 4 | 2 | 10.0 |
| B-02 | As a patient, I want to reschedule in two taps so that I avoid calling the clinic. | 4 | 4 | 3 | 5.3 |
| B-03 | As a receptionist, I want a daily schedule view so that I can manage walk-ins. | 4 | 3 | 3 | 4.0 |
| B-04 | As a doctor, I want visit notes attached to the booking so that I can prepare. | 3 | 3 | 4 | 2.3 |
| B-05 | As a patient, I want to pay online so that checkout is faster. | 3 | 2 | 5 | 1.2 |

## Acceptance criteria example (B-01)

- A reminder is sent 24 hours and 2 hours before the visit.
- The patient can confirm or cancel from the reminder.
- Cancelled slots return to the available pool.
